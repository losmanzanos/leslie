About Leslie

Compassionate, depth-oriented psychotherapy.

Leslie Cedar, LCSW, works with adults experiencing anxiety, emotional overwhelm, relational pain, self-worth struggles, and the long-term impacts of childhood trauma.

Her approach is collaborative, grounded, and deeply relational helping clients understand the patterns that developed for survival and gently create new ways of relating to themselves and others.

Therapy becomes a space to slow down, reconnect internally, and move toward a life that feels more authentic, grounded, and emotionally sustainable.

Healing begins when experiences that once felt unbearable can finally be understood with compassion.

Areas of Focus

Clinical areas of support.

Trauma

Therapeutic Approach

Therapy that honors complexity.

Healing rarely happens through pressure or performance. Therapy offers a slower, more intentional space where patterns can be explored with curiosity rather than judgment.
