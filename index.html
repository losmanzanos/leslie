About Leslie

Compassionate, depth-oriented psychotherapy.

Leslie Cedar, LCSW, has spent more than forty years helping adults heal from childhood trauma and rediscover their authentic self. She works with anxiety, depression, emotional overwhelm, relational pain, and the long-term impacts of early wounds.

Her approach is collaborative, grounded, and deeply relational — helping clients understand the patterns that developed for survival, and gently create new ways of relating to themselves and others.

Therapy becomes a space to slow down, reconnect internally, and move toward a life that feels more authentic, grounded, and emotionally sustainable.
