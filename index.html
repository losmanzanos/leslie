Recognizing the patterns

Are you holding back in your life?

You seem fine on the outside, but something inside doesn’t feel quite right.

You may:

  • feel stuck, even when you’re trying hard to move forward
  • struggle with anxiety, self-doubt, or never feeling “good enough”
  • people-please, often fearing rejection
  • feel guilt or shame when you take care of yourself
  • expect catastrophe, leaving you feeling hurt or unseen
  • push people away, or find it hard to trust
  • feel emotionally disconnected, or numb
  • have a hard time feeling joy, or simply being yourself

Even though childhood trauma can leave lasting effects long into adulthood, these patterns can change — with compassionate, depth-oriented therapy.

My approach

Therapy beyond coping.

Healing isn’t about “getting over” your past. It’s about making sense of it, reclaiming your voice, and creating the life you truly want.

Therapy is not about becoming someone else or “fixing” who you are. It’s about the relationship — one that helps you understand the experiences that shaped you, recognize the long-standing survival patterns you’ve been carrying, and reconnect with the parts of yourself that have been hidden or pushed aside.

In a safe, supportive space, therapy can help you better understand your emotions, recognize triggers connected to the past, strengthen self-awareness, develop greater self-trust, and learn to communicate your needs and boundaries more effectively.

Over time, many people begin to…

  • feel more confident and grounded
  • feel safer — within themselves and in their relationships
  • respond to life with more choice and less fear
  • reconnect with their authentic self, who they truly are

Drawing from psychodynamic, relational, self-psychology, and CBT approaches, my work goes beyond symptom relief to explore the deeper emotional experiences and relationship patterns that often begin in childhood — strengthening your sense of self, and helping you believe in yourself again.
