Questions & Answers

No silly questions.

Starting therapy can feel uncertain. These questions come up often, and I’m happy to address them directly.

How does therapy work? +
Therapy is a collaborative process where we work together at your pace. You decide what to talk about, and I listen with genuine curiosity and care. Over time, patterns emerge and you begin to understand yourself more deeply. As you feel more seen and understood, old defenses soften and new possibilities open up.
How long does therapy typically take? +
Every person is different. Some benefit from short-term focused work, while others find longer-term work allows for deeper healing. What matters most is consistency — showing up regularly creates the safety that allows real transformation.
Is everything confidential? +
Yes. What you share is confidential and protected by law. There are very limited exceptions — if I believe you’re in immediate danger, or if there’s abuse of a child or vulnerable adult. Otherwise, your privacy is my priority.
Do you offer telehealth? +
Yes. I offer both in-person sessions in Deerfield Beach and secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth throughout Florida. Many people find telehealth works well — you’re in a comfortable space and still get that one-on-one connection.
What if I’m nervous about starting? +
That’s completely normal. Many people feel nervous before their first session. The free consultation is a chance for us to talk, for you to ask questions, and to see how it feels. There’s no pressure.
How much does therapy cost? +
I’m happy to discuss fees and payment options. Please reach out: 954-370-3350 or lcedartherapy@gmail.com.
What if I’m in crisis? +
If you’re having thoughts of self-harm, please call 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or go to your nearest emergency room. You can also reach me at 954-370-3350.
