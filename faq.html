Common Questions

A few things people often ask.

Starting therapy is a courageous step. Below are the questions I’m asked most often. If something here isn’t covered, please reach out — I’m happy to answer anything.

How can therapy help me? +

In a safe and empathic space, together we will gently explore and understand how past experiences have shaped the emotional patterns that affect your life today — such as anxiety, depression, difficulty trusting others, or feeling disconnected from yourself.

Through this process, along with practical tools and strategies, you can begin to heal those emotional patterns, rebuild self-esteem, feel like your authentic self, and live with greater clarity, confidence, and connection — handling what comes your way, knowing you can take care of yourself and create the life you want.

Healing is possible. You don’t have to struggle alone — I’m here to help.

Who do you work with? +

I offer individual therapy to adult women and men of all backgrounds and cultures.

Where are you located? +

My office is in Deerfield Beach, Florida — conveniently located off I-95 and Hillsboro Blvd, with easy access from Boca Raton, Coconut Creek, Pompano Beach, and greater Fort Lauderdale.

I also offer therapy virtually throughout Florida on a secure, confidential, HIPAA-compliant platform.

What are your fees? +

There is no charge for a 30-minute consultation.

The ongoing fee is $175 for a 60-minute session.

A sliding scale fee is available in some instances.

Do you take insurance? +

I am an out-of-network provider. I will provide a superbill so you can request reimbursement from your insurance company.

Please check with your insurance about your specific out-of-network mental health benefits.

What are your hours? +

By appointment, Monday through Friday, with some availability on Saturday.

How do I schedule an appointment? +

You’re welcome to call 954-370-3350 or email lcedartherapy@gmail.com — whichever feels more comfortable.

I offer a free 30-minute consultation. Starting therapy is a courageous step; please feel free to ask any questions you have.
