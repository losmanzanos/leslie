04 · Depression Therapy

A path back to yourself.

Depression isn’t just sadness — it’s often a protective response to unbearable, unhealed pain. For many, it’s rooted in childhood trauma and emotional neglect, in past experiences that left you feeling unseen and unheard.

Request Consultation

If This Sounds Familiar

The weight of not feeling much at all.

Over time you may have learned to shut down to survive — leaving you numb, isolated, and disconnected from yourself. Feeling helpless and hopeless, you take on a heavy silence: exhausted, empty, overwhelmed, and weighed down by shame.

You may feel as if nothing will ever change. But that is far from the truth. Therapy offers a path back to yourself. Healing is possible.

How We Work

Safety first — then everything else becomes possible.

Our work begins by creating emotional safety and a trusting connection where you feel truly seen, heard, and understood. From there, change happens at a pace your system can hold.

At your pace, we will:

  • Explore the present and past roots of your depression and its effects on your daily life
  • Uncover and process emotions and memories in a supportive space
  • Shift self-blame and self-criticism into self-compassion and acceptance
  • Help you reconnect with your body and explore lifestyle supports
  • Strengthen your sense of self and improve your relationships
  • Move beyond survival — discover your true self and live the life you want
