01 · Individual Psychotherapy

A second chance to be fully met.

Therapy is a powerful opportunity — a chance to move beyond what has felt impossible or held you back. It’s a path of healing, growth, and reconnection with the real you.

Together we go at a pace that feels right to you — gradually shifting the beliefs and patterns that are holding you back, and building the awareness, self-trust, and resilience that move you forward to create meaningful change.

Request Consultation

What Brings People In

You may be carrying things you’ve never named out loud.

Maybe you’re anxious, on edge, or overwhelmed by expectations. Maybe you live with constant worry or the sense that you’re “not enough.” Maybe you feel numb, empty, shut down, or just going through the motions. Therapy is being with someone who is empathic, non-judgmental, and compassionate — where you feel safe enough to explore the patterns, experiences, and vulnerabilities that shaped you.

Trauma from childhood — emotional, physical, sexual abuse, or neglect

Anxiety, panic, constant worry, depression, grief, loss

Disconnection from self and others, numbness, feeling shut down

Boundary issues, people-pleasing, over-caretaking, co-dependency

Perfectionism, procrastination, social withdrawal, isolation

Self-criticism, self-doubt, shame, and trouble regulating stress

How We Work

Together, at the speed of trust.

I know how much courage it takes to reach out — taking this step is itself an act of strength, and it can bring great relief from struggling alone.

Our work integrates trauma-informed care, self-psychology, psychodynamic insight, and practical CBT tools — weaving understanding of the past with concrete strategies for the present.

  • Build self-trust and emotional steadiness
  • Identify and shift long-standing patterns
  • Reconnect with your needs, feelings, and values
  • Develop tools for regulation and self-soothing
  • Heal the roots, not just the symptoms
  • Strengthen your relationships and boundaries
