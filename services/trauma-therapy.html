02 · Healing & Trauma

Healing the wounds you learned to live around.

Childhood trauma — whether emotional, physical, sexual abuse, emotional neglect, or any other painful experience — shaped how you see yourself and move through the world. Therapy can gently unwind those patterns and make space for the real you.

Request Consultation

Does This Sound Familiar?

The voice that says you should be handling everything alone.

Do you often think, “I have to handle everything on my own — if I ask for help I’ll feel like I’m failing”? Or “everything has to be perfect”? Do you tell yourself you’re selfish for wanting time to yourself, that your needs don’t matter? Do you struggle with self-doubt, putting things off, or harshly criticizing yourself?

If any of these resonate, know that they aren’t the truth — they echo the fear and ingrained beliefs formed from childhood trauma. To protect yourself from threat, danger, terror, or fear, you learned to avoid closeness while still maintaining relationships — perhaps by pleasing people, overworking, numbing, fighting, fleeing, freezing, shutting down, or staying hypervigilant.

Those self-protective survival strategies were necessary. They kept you safe. But they left you feeling disconnected and alone. And as an adult, they hold you back.

How We Work Together

A relationship that feels consistently safe.

Healing happens in a relationship where you feel consistently safe and trusting. At your pace, we gently explore the protective walls that no longer serve you, process the painful wounds and memories creating havoc in your life, and make space for your own voice, feelings, needs, and values to emerge and strengthen.

You gradually integrate new understanding, strategies, and practical tools that build clarity and confidence. You start to challenge old limiting beliefs, become more aware of what you want, and feel empowered to take action.

Our work together can help you:

  • Heal the wounds and painful patterns of childhood trauma, including anxiety and depression
  • Develop an internal sense of safety and self-trust
  • Identify your triggers and separate past from present
  • Express blocked emotions and learn to regulate and self-soothe
  • Rebuild self-esteem and replace self-criticism with self-compassion
  • Set healthy boundaries and strengthen your relationships
  • Stop feeling responsible for everyone else and make space for self-care
  • Let go of perfectionism and procrastination, get unstuck
  • Become empowered to choose the life you want
