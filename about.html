A little about me

About Leslie

Over forty years of dedicated, depth-oriented therapy for adults healing from childhood trauma, anxiety, and depression.

— A Return to Inner Steadiness —

01My Journey

Four Decades of Compassionate Care

I’m a licensed clinical social worker with a deep commitment to depth-oriented psychotherapy. My journey spans more than four decades of connecting with adults through some of life’s hardest moments.

Early in my career, I worked with Veterans in Brooklyn, supporting those who had experienced significant trauma. I also spent years working with at-risk youth and adults in private practice in New York — work that gave me profound insight into resilience, survival, and the human capacity for healing.

For the past thirty years, I’ve been based in South Florida, helping adults heal from the deep wounds of childhood — the kind of wounds that don’t always look like what people expect, but that shape everything about how we move through the world.

My therapeutic approach draws from psychodynamic, relational, self-psychology, and cognitive-behavioral traditions. I believe that true healing happens in the context of a safe, compassionate relationship where all of who you are is welcome.

02Core Philosophy

What I Believe About Healing

Healing isn’t about “getting over” your past or “fixing” who you are. It’s about making sense of what shaped you, reclaiming your voice, and creating the life you truly want.

This kind of deep, meaningful change happens within a relationship — a safe space where you can slowly feel supported, emotionally seen and understood, and where you are met with empathy, respect, and compassion.

Drawing from psychodynamic, relational, self-psychology, and CBT approaches, our work together can begin to loosen old patterns and the real, true you can gently emerge.

It takes time, patience, and courage. And it’s absolutely possible — you don’t have to do it alone.

“Healing is about reclaiming our power and rewriting our future.”
