How I Help

Specialized psychotherapy for adults.

Each of these areas overlaps in real life — trauma often lives underneath anxiety, depression often grew up around unspoken pain. Wherever you start, the work moves at your pace, in a relationship where every part of you is welcome.

01 · Individual

Individual Psychotherapy

A second chance to move beyond what has felt impossible. A safe, non-judgmental space to explore the patterns and experiences that shaped your sense of self — and to gradually shift them.

Learn more →

02 · Trauma

Healing & Trauma

Childhood trauma, emotional neglect, and developmental wounds shape how you see yourself and move through the world. Our work gently loosens those protective walls so the real you can emerge.

Learn more →

03 · Anxiety

Anxiety Therapy

Persistent worry, perfectionism, panic, hypervigilance — survival strategies of a nervous system that learned the world wasn’t safe. Together we move from reactivity to grounded steadiness.

Learn more →

04 · Depression

Depression Therapy

Depression isn’t just sadness — it’s often a protective response to unbearable, unhealed pain. Therapy offers a path back to yourself, at your pace, in a space where you feel truly seen.
